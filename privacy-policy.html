Privacy Policy

At Degree to Career, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your information when you interact with our website or submit your details through our enquiry forms.

Information We Collect

We may collect information including:

  • Name

  • Email Address

  • Educational Qualification
  • Work Experience (if applicable)
  • Course Preferences
  • Any information voluntarily shared by you

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used for:

  • Providing education counselling

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Where necessary and with your enquiry, your information may be shared with universities or authorised education partners only for admission-related purposes.

Your Consent

By using this website or submitting your information through our enquiry forms, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

Policy Updates
