Privacy Policy

Degree to Career respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information voluntarily provided when you contact us, submit an enquiry or request education guidance.

Information We May Collect

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • City or location
  • Educational qualification
  • Course or programme preference
  • Other information voluntarily submitted through an enquiry

How We Use Information

We may use submitted information to respond to enquiries, provide programme and university information, arrange counselling, assist with admission-related requests and improve our services.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Where relevant to an enquiry, information may be shared with the concerned university or authorised education partner for admission-related communication or assistance.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to university and third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Consent

By submitting information through our website or enquiry forms, you consent to its use for the purposes described in this policy and to being contacted regarding your enquiry.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
